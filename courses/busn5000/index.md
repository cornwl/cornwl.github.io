    <section class="hub-section hub-section--about">
      <div class="hub-shell hub-about">
        <div class="hub-section__intro">
          <p class="hub-section__label">About the course</p>
          <h2>From information<br>to insight</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="hub-about__copy">
          <p class="hub-lead">The modern world is awash in a seemingly unlimited amount of data. Harnessing these data for decision-making begins with acquiring the raw information and ends with communicating the results of analysis.</p>
          <p>Along the way, data must be transformed for analysis and statistical methods matched to the task at hand. BUSN 5000 develops the skills needed at every stage of that value chain: data transformation; descriptive, explanatory, and predictive analysis; and professional communication. We organize that work as ATAC: acquire, transform, analyze, communicate.</p>
          <div class="hub-pillars" aria-label="ATAC analysis pipeline">
            <span>Acquire</span>
            <span>Transform</span>
            <span>Analyze</span>
            <span>Communicate</span>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="hub-section hub-section--outcomes">
      <div class="hub-shell">
        <div class="hub-section__heading">
          <div>
            <p class="hub-section__label">Course objectives</p>
            <h2>What you will learn</h2>
          </div>
          <p>By the end of the course, you should understand how to move deliberately from a business or policy question to a reproducible analysis and a clear account of what the evidence shows.</p>
        </div>
        <ol class="hub-outcome-list">
          <li>Acquire and prepare data for analysis.</li>
          <li>Design reproducible data analyses.</li>
          <li>Map business problems and policy questions to hypotheses about relationships in data.</li>
          <li>Describe data and perform basic descriptive analysis.</li>
          <li>Implement and interpret basic causal-inference research designs.</li>
          <li>Implement and interpret basic machine-learning algorithms.</li>
          <li>Communicate results from descriptive, causal, and predictive analyses.</li>
        </ol>
      </div>
    </section>
